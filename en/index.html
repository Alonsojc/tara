Frequently Asked Questions

What you need to know

What documents do I need to exchange currency?

It depends on the transaction amount:

  • Menos de $999 USD (o equivalente): No identification required.
  • De $1,000 a $4,999 USD: Valid official photo ID (INE, passport or professional license).
  • De $5,000 a $10,000 USD: Official ID, recent proof of address and questionnaire about the source of funds.
How much can I exchange per day?

The daily limit is $10,000 USD (or its equivalent in other currencies) per person. The monthly limit is $100,000 USD or equivalent, in accordance with current regulations.

Do you accept card payments?

No. By regulation, currency exchange houses operate exclusively in cash. Credit cards, debit cards and electronic transfers are not accepted.

What currencies do you trade?

Compramos y vendemos: US Dollar (USD), Euro (EUR), Canadian Dollar (CAD), Australian Dollar (AUD), British Pound (GBP), Swiss Franc (CHF), Japanese Yen (JPY) y Chinese Yuan (CNY). También compramos y vendemos monedas de oro y plata.

What is the minimum age to make transactions?

You must be at least 18 years old and present a valid official ID for amounts that require it.

Is the exchange rate on the website the same as at the branch?

The exchange rate shown on our website is the interbank reference rate and does not constitute a binding offer. The applicable rate will be the one in effect at the time of your transaction at the branch. We recommend checking via WhatsApp before your visit.
